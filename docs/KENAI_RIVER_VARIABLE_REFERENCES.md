# Kenai River Condition Calculation Variable References

Last updated: 2026-05-07

## Overview

This file documents Kenai River-specific weather, river, tide, moon, regulation, access-pressure, and local-activity variables that are seeded into the TP information database, interpreted here as the local SQLite `condition_variables` table initialized by `src/kenai_engine/db.py`.

The variables below are prepared for condition calculations. They do not change current scoring behavior unless the existing engine already reads an equivalent input. Direct measurements should be preferred over proxies. Local activity and social variables must remain aggregate-only, manually supplied, or collected through approved public APIs.

## Calculation and Proxy Notes

- Direct or calculated from official APIs: `hours_after_hot_sunny_day`, `morning_after_clear_cold_night`, `rainfall_48hr_in_tributary_basins`, `river_stage_velocity_change`, `recent_regulation_change_flag`, `hours_since_emergency_order_update`, `tide_plus_wind_alignment`, `moon_phase_plus_tide_range_interaction`, `sun_angle_at_fishing_time`.
- Inferred proxies from official data: `estimated_water_clarity_from_flow_plus_recent_rain`, `commercial_opening_nearby_flag`.
- Manual or approved-aggregate proxy only: `guide_boat_density_proxy`, `parking_pressure_proxy`, `bait_shop_sentiment_score`, `fish_cleaning_station_activity`.
- Unavailable for safe automated collection without an approved API/source: `local_facebook_fish_photo_count`.

## Scraping and Compliance Notes

- API-first sources: NWS `api.weather.gov`, USGS Water Services, NOAA CO-OPS, and local solar-position calculation from documented NOAA methods.
- Public page sources: ADF&G emergency orders, ADF&G fishing reports, ADF&G commercial fishery news, City of Kenai pages, Alaska DNR KRSMA pages, Alaska DEC Kenai River water quality page.
- Manual-input variables: guide boat density, parking pressure when no live feed exists, bait-shop sentiment when no approved report feed exists, Facebook photo count, and fish cleaning station activity.
- Privacy-sensitive variables store aggregate counts or scores only. Do not collect names, comments, profile links, private posts, login-gated content, or restricted camera imagery.
- HTML scraping must respect robots.txt, rate limits, and source terms. Prefer official APIs, downloadable data, RSS, CSV, JSON, or manual curation.

## Assumptions

- “TP information database” means the local SQLite `condition_variables` metadata table.
- The modeled area is the Kenai River, with lower-river/mouth variables tied to the existing NOAA tide station `9455742`.
- Existing USGS gauge assumptions follow project config: `15258000`, `15266010`, `15266110`, and `15266300`.
- Weather inputs should use NWS gridpoints for selected Kenai River access coordinates, especially Kenai mouth, Soldotna, Russian River/Cooper Landing, Moose River/Sterling, and Funny River/Soldotna context points.
- Runtime timestamps should remain UTC internally; threshold windows that say “morning,” “overnight,” or “day” should be interpreted in Alaska local time.
- Proposed thresholds: hot day >=70 F, sunny sky cover <=25%, cold night <=40 F, clear night sky cover <=25%, recent rain window 48 hours, recent regulation-change window 72 hours.
- “Nearby commercial opening” means configured Upper Cook Inlet or other explicit ADF&G management areas near enough to be relevant to Kenai salmon planning context.
- Social/local activity variables are proxy-only and must not be treated as official truth.
